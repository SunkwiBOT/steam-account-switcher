## Inter (font)

- The variable font from the npm package `@fontsource-variable/inter`, bundled and
  self-hosted (no remote request).
- Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, see the package's own `LICENSE` file; not

## shadcn-svelte and bits-ui (UI components)

- The dialog, dropdown menu, context menu, switch, tabs, button and checkbox
  components under `ui/src/lib/components/ui/` are generated by the `shadcn-svelte`
  CLI from the shadcn-svelte registry and built on the `bits-ui` headless primitives,
  then adapted to this app's styling.
- Both are under the MIT licence; see their own packages (`bits-ui` in
  `package-lock.json`) for the licence text.

## Rank artwork

`ui/public/ranks/cs2/`, `ui/public/ranks/marvel-rivals/` and `ui/public/ranks/overwatch/`
contain rank-tier icons from Counter-Strike 2 (Valve), Marvel Rivals (NetEase Games) and
Overwatch (Blizzard Entertainment / Microsoft), used here only to let the app show an
account's rank for that game. These images, and the game names and marks, are the
property of their respective publishers and are not covered by this project's MIT
licence; they are not original work of this project.
